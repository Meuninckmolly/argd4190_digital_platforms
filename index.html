Molly Meuninck Designs

Motivated third-year Graphic Design student at the University of Georgia dedicated to blending compelling visual storytelling with strong technical proficiency. As a Student Assistant Graphic Designer for the Hargrett Library, I contribute creative solutions that support archival, educational, and promotional initiatives while refining my expertise in branding, layout design, and digital media production. Complementing my academic and professional experience, I am pursuing a New Media Certificate (Dev-Track) and an Entrepreneurship Certificate, focusing my work on the intersection of design, emerging digital technologies, and innovative business strategy. I'm passionate about creating impactful, user-centered experiences. I combine creativity, technology, and entrepreneurial thinking to develop forward-thinking design solutions.
